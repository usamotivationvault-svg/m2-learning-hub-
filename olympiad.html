SOF Olympiads

IMO (International Mathematics Olympiad)

Class 1–12 ke students ke liye. Logical reasoning + advanced maths. Exam Pattern: Reasoning + Maths + Achievers Section.

NSO (National Science Olympiad)

Science Olympiad covering Physics, Chemistry, Biology. Concept-based questions with real-world applications.

IOQM (Indian Olympiad Qualifier in Mathematics)

High-level mathematics competition. Class 8–12 ke liye. Problem solving + logical thinking.

Exam Type: Objective + Integer answers Focus: Olympiad level questions

NSEJS (Junior Science Olympiad)

Class 8–10 ke liye National level science exam.

Subjects: Physics, Chemistry, Biology Level: Conceptual + analytical
