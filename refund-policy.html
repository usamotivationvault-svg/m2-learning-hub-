Refund Policy

Please read this policy carefully before enrolling. We believe in full transparency about our fee structure.

Last updated: June 2026
📌

No Refunds After Enrollment

M² Learning Hub maintains a strict no-refund policy once a student has been enrolled and fees have been paid. We strongly encourage all prospective students to attend a free demo class before making any payment decision.

01

Our Fee Policy

At M² Learning Hub, we invest significant time and resources in preparing personalised content, maintaining small batches, and ensuring each student receives dedicated attention from our expert faculty. To protect the quality and sustainability of our services:

  • All course fees, once paid, are final and non-refundable
  • Fees are not transferable to another student, batch, or course period
  • Partial refunds are not offered under any circumstance after enrollment

💡 We offer a free demo class before enrollment. This gives you the opportunity to experience our teaching style, faculty, and batch structure before committing financially. Please make use of this before enrolling.

02

Refund Policy at a Glance

Situation Refund Applicable?

Student misses classes / poor attendance No Refund
Student dissatisfied with teaching style (post-enrollment) No Refund

03

Cancellation Before Enrollment

If you have submitted a fee payment but enrollment has not yet been formally confirmed by M² Learning Hub, you may contact us within 24 hours to request a cancellation. Such requests will be considered on a case-by-case basis.

To raise this, contact us immediately on WhatsApp at +91 88825 65496.

04

Makeup Classes for Cancelled Sessions

In the event that M² Learning Hub has to cancel a scheduled session due to faculty unavailability, technical issues, or public holidays, we will:

  • Inform students in advance via WhatsApp wherever possible
  • Arrange a makeup/compensatory class at a mutually agreed time
  • Not issue monetary refunds for such cancellations
05

Duplicate Payment Refunds

If a payment is accidentally made twice for the same enrollment (duplicate transaction), a full refund of the duplicate amount will be processed within 5–7 working days upon verification. Please contact us on WhatsApp with the payment proof to initiate this process.

06

Why We Maintain This Policy

We maintain this policy because:

  • Batch sizes are kept small and seats are limited — holding a seat for a paying student means others are turned away
  • Faculty preparation, study material creation, and scheduling are done in advance
  • Our free demo ensures students can make an informed decision before committing

We encourage all students and parents to attend the free demo session, ask any questions they have, and be fully satisfied before paying the course fee.

07

Changes to This Policy

M² Learning Hub reserves the right to revise this Refund Policy at any time. Any updates will be reflected on this page with a revised date. The policy applicable at the time of your enrollment shall govern your enrollment.
