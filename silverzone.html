Popular SilverZone Olympiads

SilverZone Foundation is a veteran in the field of Olympiads, providing a global platform for students to compete in various subjects. Key exams include:

  • iOM: International Olympiad of Mathematics
  • iOS: International Olympiad of Science
  • iOEL: International Olympiad of English Language
  • SKGK: Smart Kid General Knowledge Olympiad

Eligibility & Levels

The SilverZone Foundation offers a structured 3-level progression to identify and reward global talent.

  • Eligibility: Students from Class 1 to 12 can participate.
  • Level 1: All students of classes 1 to 12 are eligible.
  • Level 2: Top 5% of participants from Level 1, plus top rank holders in each zone and school (for classes 3-12).
  • Level 3: International Level – held in New Delhi for the top rank holders of Level 2.

Exam Pattern & Structure

1 Hour Duration
35 / 50 MCQ Questions

Offline/Online Mode

Question Breakdown:

  • Classes 1-4: 35 Questions (Total 100 Marks).
  • Classes 5-12: 50 Questions (Total 100 Marks).
  • Sections: Includes Subject-Specific content, Logical Reasoning, and the 'Achievers Section'.

Syllabus & Awards

The syllabus is aligned with CBSE, ICSE, and various State Boards, with an additional emphasis on analytical thinking and problem-solving.

Prizes & Recognition:

🥇 Cash Prizes, Gold, Silver, and Bronze Medals for Zonal & International toppers.
📜 Participation Certificates for all students.
📈 Comprehensive Student Performance Report (SPR).
