About NSEJS

The National Standard Examination in Junior Science (NSEJS) is a national-level science competition organized by the IAPT (Indian Association of Physics Teachers). It is the first stage towards the International Junior Science Olympiad (IJSO).

Held annually in November, the exam covers integrated Physics, Chemistry, and Biology, challenging students to apply scientific principles to complex problems.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Age Group: Typically for students born between specific dates (e.g., Jan 1, 2011 to Dec 31, 2012 for the current cycle).
  • Current Grade: Students studying in Class 10 or below.
  • Registration: Usually done through schools, with official dates announced on the IAPT website.

Exam Pattern & Marking

2 Hours Duration
60 - 80 MCQ Questions
+3 / -1 Marking Scheme
English/Hindi Medium

The paper is designed to test analytical thinking rather than rote memorization. Correct answers usually carry +3 marks, while incorrect answers result in a -1 penalty.

Selection Process (Stages)

1. NSEJS (First Round) Conducted by IAPT in November. Top scorers qualify for the next level.
2. INJSO (Second Round) The Indian National Junior Science Olympiad, held in January for the top ~300 students.
3. OCSC Orientation-Cum-Selection Camp for the top ~35 students in the country.
4. IJSO (Final Stage) The International Junior Science Olympiad, where the selected Indian team competes globally.
