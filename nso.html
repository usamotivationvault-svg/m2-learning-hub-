Multiple-choice questions (MCQs) in English focusing on Science & Logic.

What is the NSO?

The National Science Olympiad (NSO) is organized by the Science Olympiad Foundation (SOF). Its primary purpose is to cultivate scientific aptitude, enhance critical thinking, and provide competitive exposure to young students.

Scientific Skill Development: Shifts learning from rote memorization to practical, inquiry-based thinking.

Comprehensive Curriculum: Strictly aligns with CBSE, ICSE/ISC, and major state board curricula.

Global Recognition: Provides a platform to gauge performance and gain recognition on a national and international level.

Core Sections

  • 1 Logical Reasoning
  • 2 Science
  • 3 Achievers Section (HOTS)

Exam Pattern & Structure

Class Group Total Questions Time Format
Classes 1 to 4 35 MCQs 60 Minutes Logical Reasoning, Science, Achievers
Classes 5 to 12 50 MCQs 60 Minutes Logical Reasoning, Science, Achievers

The Achievers Section

This section contains Higher Order Thinking Skills (HOTS) questions. It is designed to test a student's ability to apply scientific concepts to complex scenarios and carries higher weightage.

Level 2 Qualifiers

For Classes 3-12, qualification for Level 2 requires being in the Top 5% internationally (class-wise), Top 25 rank holders (zone-wise), or a class topper (with at least 10 participating students).

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the syllabus for the NSO?
The content strictly follows the curricula established by CBSE, ICSE/ISC, and recognized state boards for the respective classes.
Do Classes 1 and 2 have a Level 2 exam?
No, students in Classes 1 and 2 are judged solely based on their performance in the single-level Level 1 exam.
How can I register for the NSO?
Registration is strictly through schools registered with SOF. Individual registrations are not accepted. Please contact your school's coordinating teacher to sign up.
